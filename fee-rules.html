How a fee is decided

A fee belongs to a group, not to a student. When a payment or a reminder needs an amount, the academy's rules are checked from the most specific to the least: a rate set on one student first, then their batch, then a rule for everyone. The first one that fits wins.

So changing the Morning rule changes it for everyone in Morning, immediately, including what tomorrow's reminders say. Nothing needs re-entering student by student.

To give one student their own rate — a sibling discount, a scholarship — set it on that student on the Members page. That beats every rule here, which is why those students are counted separately above.
