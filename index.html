The venue

What you get at VAKAMAN FE

Small, honest list. Everything here is simply what is on the ground today.

01

Two surfaces, one price

Two Astro nets and two Matting nets. The surface is yours to pick when you arrive — it does not change what the hour costs.

02

A ground you get to yourself

Full-day hire means the ground is booked to one team for the day. Club matches and tournament days are not squeezed around other bookings.

03

Coaching and facilities together

Members train in age-group batches at the same venue they can book a net at, so extra practice does not mean travelling somewhere else.

04

Bookings and fees on record

Every booking and every fee is recorded against your name, and the amount is the one the academy's system set — so what you are told and what is in the books are the same number.
