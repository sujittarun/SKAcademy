An application is not a member. It is an enquiry a family filed on the public form. Approving one is what creates the member, the enrolment and the fee rule — in a single call to the database — and stamps the new member back onto the application. Until then the Members tab is correctly empty: nobody has been admitted. Declining does not delete anything; the enquiry stays, because "did we ever reply to that family" is a real question and because the per-phone limit on the public form counts these rows. An application whose status is neither pending, approved nor declined appears under All only.
